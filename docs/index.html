01 / WHY FUMI

AI doesn't
have to live
in a tab.

FUMI turns an AI into something you can actually see and touch. A tiny screen becomes its face. A microphone lets it listen. A speaker lets it answer.

That's it. No giant robot. No sci-fi lab. Just a little buddy that happens to run on a tiny board.
