01 / THE IDEA

AI shouldn't only live in a browser.

FUMI turns an AI agent into something physical: a small character you can touch, talk to, feed, play with, and keep around. The hardware is simple on purpose — the interesting part is what you build on top of it.

02 / WHAT FUMI DOES01—06

Not just a chat box.

FUMI combines a living on-device interface with cloud AI. Its state can change over time, while touch and voice turn the little display into an actual companion.

01Animated character UIExpressions, idle states and reactions
02Touch interactionInteract directly with the display
03Life systemHunger, energy, happiness, bond & growth
04Mini-gameA small game loop inside the companion
05Push-to-talk voiceSpeak to FUMI through the onboard mic
06Cloud AI chatProvider-agnostic model backend

HARDWARE OPTIONS

One brain. Different builds.

The Waveshare board is the current reference build. Other ESP32-S3 setups are possible, but need display, touch and audio driver adaptation.

Important: these are hardware paths, not plug-and-play firmware targets. The current firmware is built around the Waveshare ESP32-S3 Touch LCD 1.46″.

06 / BRING YOUR OWN BRAIN

The device doesn't care which model you use.

FUMI separates the physical device from the AI provider. Switch the text model in the backend instead of rebuilding the firmware every time you want to experiment.
