01
THE IDEA

Why put an AI
inside a device?

I wanted to build an AI companion that actually lives on a physical device instead of existing only in a browser or terminal. A physical device changes the interaction completely: it has a screen, a body, limited memory, real input/output constraints, and has to be useful even when the interface is just a small touch display.

FUMI started from that idea — building a small desktop companion and figuring out what it takes to make an AI feel like part of a device rather than just another chat interface.

02
THE DEVICE

Small hardware.
Real constraints.

FUMI runs on an ESP32-S3 with a round touch display. The firmware uses LVGL for expressions, animations, touch interaction, and companion state.

IN ACTION

Touch. Talk. Listen.

The voice path is implemented end-to-end. The device captures audio, sends it through speech-to-text and the AI backend, then turns the response back into speech.
